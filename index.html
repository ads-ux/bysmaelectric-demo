About Sam

Guelph's Electrician.
Not a Franchise. Not a Call Centre.

"Bysma Electric started with a simple goal: do great work and treat people right. Since then, I've had the privilege of working in countless homes and businesses, building relationships that mean just as much to me as the work itself."

Sam Bysma started Bysma Electric 13 years ago with a straightforward idea: do good work, charge a fair price, and treat people's homes the way you'd want your own treated. That hasn't changed.

When you call Bysma Electric, Sam is the one who shows up. Not a crew of subcontractors you've never met — Sam himself. He's a certified Master Electrician, the highest level of electrical licensing in Ontario, and he's been putting that to work across Guelph since 2013.

In 13 years he's worked in every corner of this city — the century homes on Edinburgh with their knob-and-tube wiring, the 1980s bungalows in the West End that need panel upgrades, and the brand new builds going up in Clairfields and Westminster Woods. He knows what Guelph's homes need because he's worked in all of them.

Why Experience Matters

Every Guelph Home
Is a Little Different.

Guelph is an unusual city electrically. You've got 100-year-old stone homes downtown with wiring that predates modern code, postwar bungalows in the West End running on 60-amp service, and massive new developments on the south end all going in at once. There's no one-size-fits-all approach.

That's why experience matters. Sam has worked in all of them. He knows what to look for, what the ESA inspector will flag, and how to get the job done right the first time — whether it's a heritage home on Waterloo Ave or a new build in Pergola Commons.

Talk to Sam About Your Home
🏛️
Downtown & Heritage Homes
Century homes on Waterloo Ave, Edinburgh Rd, and the Ward often have knob-and-tube or aluminum wiring. Sam knows how to bring them up to code safely and affordably.
🏠
West End & Postwar Bungalows
Homes built in the '60s–'80s are regularly running on 60-amp or 100-amp panels that can't handle modern loads. Panel upgrades are one of Sam's most common Guelph jobs.
🏗️
New Builds — South End & Clairfields
New construction in Kortright Hills, Westminster Woods, and Pergola Commons requires rough-in wiring, service connections, and ESA inspection. Sam works with builders and direct with homeowners.
🏢
Commercial & Light Industrial
Guelph has a strong commercial and manufacturing base. Sam handles office fit-outs, retail spaces, and light industrial electrical — permitted and to code.

What Sam Does

Residential and commercial electrical work — all permitted, all to ESA standards, all done by Sam himself.

🔌

Panel Upgrades

Upgrading from 60-amp or 100-amp to 200-amp service. Essential for EV chargers, hot tubs, or older Guelph homes that need more capacity. Fully permitted.

🏠

Residential Electrical

Renovations, additions, new construction wiring, and repairs. Sam has worked in every neighbourhood in Guelph — old homes and new builds alike.

🚗

EV Charger Installation

Level 2 home charging for all makes and models. Includes circuit assessment, panel check, ESA permit, and inspection — done properly.

💡

Pot Lights & Lighting

Recessed pot lights, dimmer switches, outdoor lighting, under-cabinet strips. Installed cleanly — minimal ceiling damage, no mess left behind.

🧱

Knob & Tube / Old Wiring

Common in Guelph's downtown and older West End homes. Sam has updated hundreds of them — he knows what insurers require and what the ESA inspector looks for.

🏢

Commercial Electrical

Office fit-outs, retail and warehouse electrical, light industrial. Guelph's business community has trusted Sam for 13+ years.
